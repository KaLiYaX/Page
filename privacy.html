Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the KxPage content uploader ("the Tool") collects and how it is used. The Tool is a single-owner, invite-only application — it does not collect data from the general public.

Data we collect

DataWhy we collect it
TikTok account identifier (open_id) and display nameTo know which TikTok account to publish to
TikTok access & refresh tokensTo authenticate API requests to TikTok on the owner's behalf
Video files and captions submitted by the ownerTo upload them to TikTok
Basic upload logs (timestamps, publish status)To show upload history and troubleshoot failures

How data is stored

Tokens and upload history are stored locally on the owner's own server/device. They are not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Video files are deleted from local storage once a video has been successfully sent to TikTok.

How data is used

Data is used exclusively to operate the Tool: authenticating with TikTok, uploading the owner's own content, and reporting status back to the owner. No data is shared with third parties other than TikTok, which is required to publish the content.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the Tool's access to their TikTok account at any time from TikTok's app-permissions settings. Doing so immediately invalidates the Tool's stored tokens.

Data retention

Tokens are retained until the owner disconnects the account or requests deletion. Upload history is retained to maintain a record of what was posted and can be cleared on request.

Children's privacy

The Tool is not directed at children and is not used to collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
