KxPage

Content Uploader for TikTok

A personal tool that publishes a page owner's own video content to their TikTok profile.

What this does

KxPage is a private tool used by a single content page owner to publish their own videos to their TikTok account. The owner receives finished video files (with the rights or permission to publish them), reviews them, and sends them through the tool to their connected TikTok profile.

How TikTok is used

  1. Login Kit — the account owner signs in with their own TikTok account and authorizes the connection. No credentials are stored; TikTok's OAuth flow issues an access token that the owner can revoke at any time from their TikTok settings.
  2. Content Posting API (Direct Post) — after authorization, the owner selects a video and an optional caption. The tool uploads the file directly to TikTok on the owner's behalf and reports back the publish status.

Who uses it

This is a single-owner, invite-only tool. It is not a public multi-user platform — only the verified account owner can trigger uploads.
